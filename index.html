About me

Who am I?

I'm a Data Analyst who owns analytics end to end: pulling data through REST APIs, cleaning it with ETL scripts, modeling it, and designing the dashboards that operations and management use every day.

At Chartered Speed Ltd. I have built 10+ Qlik Sense Cloud applications across fleet monitoring, fuel efficiency, trips, driver behaviour, alerts, tickets and collections, replacing manual reporting with live, automatically refreshed views.

Before this I automated research reporting with Python and Excel at Magneto IT Solutions, and learned Power BI, Tableau and SQL at Grras Solutions through end-to-end projects.

I hold a B.Tech in Computer Science and keep growing into the cloud side of data: Microsoft Fabric, Azure Data Factory and Databricks.
